Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

The Short Version: Secret Santa collects only what it needs to run your gift exchange: your name, your gift profile and wish list, your groups, and the anonymous questions you send. Only your Santa sees your gift profile. We never sell your information or use it for advertising or tracking, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Secret Santa app ("the App") for iPhone handles your information. The App is made by an independent developer ("we", "us").

Information We Collect

  • Your name, from Sign in with Apple or what you type, so your group knows who you are. We don't receive your email address unless you choose to share it with Apple's sign-in, and we don't store it.
  • Your gift profile and wish lists: things you love, favorite colors, dislikes, sizes, and the wish list items and links you add.
  • Group details you create or join: the group name, exchange date, location, budget, members and exclusions.
  • Anonymous questions between you and your Santa or giftee. We don't record who sent a question.
  • Reports: if you report a conversation, we keep a copy of the other person's recent messages in it, along with who made the report and who was reported, so we can review it. Only we see reports; the other person isn't told who reported them. We keep a report only as long as we need it to review and act on it.
  • A push notification token for your device, so we can send reminders and match updates.

Information We Don't Collect

  • Your email address or phone number
  • Your device's location, contacts, photos or camera images
  • Usage analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking data

Who Can See It

Members of a group see each other's names. Only your Santa sees your gift profile and wish list. Nobody can see who drew whom, except the organizer when they turned on Let me see all matches before the draw, and everyone in the group is shown when that's on.

Where It's Stored

Your information is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging) in the United States. Google's handling of this data is described in the Firebase privacy and security page. Sign-in is handled by Apple, as described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Tips

Tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Third-Party Services

Apart from Google Firebase and Apple, the App uses no third-party services. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and we never sell your information.

Children's Privacy

The App isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting Your Data

You can leave a group at any time from the group screen. To delete everything, tap Me → Delete account in the App. That removes your profile, wish lists, questions and memberships right away. If names were already drawn, your Santa is given someone new.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be shown by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a future version of the App handles information differently, we will update this policy before that version is released.

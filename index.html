About the App

Secret Santa runs your gift exchange for you. Start a group for your family, friends or office, invite everyone with a code, and draw names with one tap. Everyone sees only their own match. It runs on iPhone (iOS 26.5 or later), and you sign in with your Apple Account.

Groups

Set a budget and an exchange date, then share the 6-character invite code. Be in as many groups as you like.

The draw

Draw names when everyone's in. Each person unwraps their own tag, and only they know who they got.

Gift profiles

Add what you love, your favorite colors, your sizes and a few wish list ideas. Your Santa sees them.

Ask

Message your giftee to check a size or a color. They can answer, but they won't see who's asking.

Community Rules

Secret Santa is for friendly gift exchanges. By using the app you agree not to send abusive, harassing, hateful, sexual or otherwise objectionable messages. We review every report within 24 hours and remove offending content and users who break these rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a Secret Santa?

Tap + on the home screen, choose Create a Secret Santa, give your group a name, and set a budget and exchange date. Then tap Invite people to share the invite code. You're the organizer, so you're the one who draws names.

How do I join a group?

Ask your organizer for the 6-character invite code. Tap +, choose Join with a code, and enter the code or paste their invite. Groups can't be joined after names are drawn.

How many people do I need?

At least 3. In very small groups the draw still stays hidden, but people may be able to work out who has whom, so the app lets you know.

Can I keep two people from drawing each other?

Yes. Before the draw, the organizer can add exclusions from the group screen. Pick two people and they won't draw each other, in either direction. It's handy for couples and roommates. If there are too many exclusions to make a fair draw, the app tells you instead of drawing.

Can the organizer see who has whom?

Only if they turn on Let me see all matches when setting up the group, and everyone in the group is shown that it's on. Otherwise nobody can see the matches, not even the organizer.

Can we redraw names?

No. Once names are drawn the draw is final, which keeps anyone from fishing for a different match. If you need to start over, the organizer can delete the group and make a new one.

Are messages really anonymous?

Yes. When you ask your giftee a question, they see it as coming from "your Santa". Your name is never attached, and the app doesn't record who sent it.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the conversation and tap ··· in the top corner. Block stops that person from sending you messages; you can unblock from the same menu. Report sends their messages to us for review and blocks them. They aren't told who reported them, and blocking or reporting your Santa never reveals who they are.

Someone left after names were drawn. What happens?

Their Santa is given someone new, so nobody is left without a person to buy for.

I'm not getting notifications.

Open the Settings app, tap Notifications → Secret Santa, and make sure Allow Notifications is on. The app sends reminders before the exchange and lets you know when names are drawn, when your match changes, and when you get a new message.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap Me → Delete account and confirm with Apple. Your profile, wish lists and messages are deleted right away, and you're removed from every group. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What does the tip jar do?

Nothing extra. Secret Santa is made by one person, and a tip helps keep it running. Tips are one-time purchases through Apple and are completely optional.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please email dsimpsonseeds@gmail.com with what happened, what you expected, and your iPhone model and iOS version. A screenshot helps a lot. Thank you!
